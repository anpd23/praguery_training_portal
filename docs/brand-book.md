# Brand Book — training excerpt

Pulled from *Our Brilliant Brand Book* for Academy copy and quizzes.

## Purpose
Make life just a little bit sweeter.

## Taglines
- Hand crafted with sweetness
- A twist on tradition
- Sign-off: Making life just a little bit sweeter for friends & family

## Voice
Warm & Relaxed for instruction. Energetic & Celebratory for milestones (path complete, certificate earned).

## Vocab
Never minimize the craft. Never “it’s just.” Guests, not customers, in guest-facing lines. House-made, freshly baked, hand-rolled. No discount language.

## Chimney cake — staff version
It’s a European pastry we bake fresh right here, just for you. Crunchy on the outside, soft on the inside, and we fill it with our house-made ice cream and sauces. It’s our specialty.
